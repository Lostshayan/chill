Frequently Asked Questions
Is Lofify completely free?
Yes! Lofify is 100% free, open-source, and has no paywalls or subscription tiers.
Can I paste any YouTube link into the custom radio?
Yes, you can paste standard YouTube video URLs, live lo-fi stream links, or playlist links. Playback will synchronize across all peers in your study room.
How do I delete my profile data?
Open your Profile & Analytics panel via the TV bezel button, scroll to the bottom, and click "Delete Account". All records are immediately and permanently erased from our Supabase database.
